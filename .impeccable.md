# Impeccable Design Context: VietDub / Anime AI Voice

## Target Audience
- Anime fans, VTuber enthusiasts, and internet culture creators.
- Tech-savvy users who want high-quality AI voice covers for their favorite anime characters.
- Users who appreciate strong visual themes and "waifu" aesthetics.

## Use Cases
- Selecting a voice model (Kurumi, Elaina, Miku, Mirai).
- Uploading an audio file or pasting a YouTube link.
- Extracting vocals and synthesizing them into the selected character's voice.
- Listening to the generated audio with a beautiful audio visualizer.
- Downloading or sharing the final result.

## Brand Personality & Tone
- **Tone**: "Elegant Gothic Anime" meets "Cyberpunk Hacker". Dark, immersive, slightly mysterious, but highly polished. 
- **Personality**: Playful but powerful. The UI should feel like a high-end magical grimoire or a secret anime hacker terminal.
- **Differentiation**: Instead of a generic dashboard, it should feel like you are directly interacting with the characters. Distinctive micro-animations for interactions, particle effects (already implemented for characters), and deep, rich colors.

## Design Direction
- **Typography**: Move away from generic sans-serifs as the primary font for everything. Use a highly stylized, elegant serif (like `Cormorant Garamond` or `Cinzel`) for headings to give that "Gothic / Magical" feel, paired with a hyper-legible geometric sans (like `Outfit` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`) for UI elements. No generic `Inter` or `Arial`.
- **Color Palette**: Very dark, rich backgrounds (near pitch black with deep crimson or violet undertones). Accent colors should be vibrant and glowing (neon pinks, blood reds, electric blues) to stand out against the dark. Avoid generic gray borders; use tinted, low-opacity brand colors.
- **Layout & Spatial**: Break out of the "standard card grid" box. Use asymmetrical layouts, overlapping elements, or glassmorphism *only where it makes sense* (e.g., character selection floating over the background). 
- **Motion**: Fluid, purposeful motion. Easing should be dramatic (fast snap, slow settle) rather than bouncy. Hover states should feel alive (glowing text, shifting backgrounds).
